How many rings would LeBron have won with Jordan's teammates? I simulated it one hundred thousand times. Jordan had Pippen, Rodman and Phil Jackson. LeBron fans say give him that team and he wins just as many. So I measured how much every player adds per one hundred possessions, using RAPTOR, FiveThirtyEight's player rating. At their best, Jordan and LeBron both added about eight points. Then I took Jordan out of every Bulls season from nineteen eighty-five to nineteen ninety-eight, put LeBron in at the same age, and replayed every playoff run against the real opponents.
